CV-2x3-140

Agreement on an add-on view to the coordination view to include space boundaries 

Based on          IFC2x3

Initiator            ISG Meeting-Helsinki

Effects              Extended Coordination View

Date                   2007 05 23

Description:

The export of space boundaries is an add-on functionality that can be provided on top of the extended coordination view. The exact definition of space boundaries is included in the model view definition for 1st level space boundaries.

An additional validation / certification for supporting 1st level space boundaries may be organized, it will not be validated as part of the existing extended coordination view.

Currently the 1st level space boundaries are an export-only requirement for an add-on to the extended coordination view. Other BIM/CAD applications are not required to import space boundaries, they can be ignored on import.
