4.1.5.14 Station

Station for element template. An IfcElement may have a linear referencing measurement attached to it. The IfcReferent.Name features a localized form of station measurement encodings and shall be positioned on an IfcAlignment to ensure that a distance along measurement is clearly defined. The IfcReferent should have a placement.
