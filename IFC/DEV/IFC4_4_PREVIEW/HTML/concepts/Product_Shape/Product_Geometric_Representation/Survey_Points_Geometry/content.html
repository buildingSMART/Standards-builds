4.1.7.1.14 Survey Points Geometry

Elements may provide a 'SurveyPoints' representation for defining a contour for IfcSite. It contains the survey points as a set of Cartesian points and optionally breaklines.

The representation identifier and type and the only allowed single representation item of the 'SurveyPoints' representation are:

IfcShapeRepresentation.RepresentationIdentifier = 'SurveyPoints' IfcShapeRepresentation.RepresentationType : 'GeometricCurveSet' IfcShapeRepresentation.Items = IfcGeometricCurveSet
