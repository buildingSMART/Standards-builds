8.10.4.9.1 Semantic definition

A set of extended mechanical properties related to steel (or other metallic and isotropic) materials.

8.10.4.9.2 Applicable entities

PSET_MATERIALDRIVEN The property sets defined by this IfcPropertySetTemplate are to be encoded in an IfcMaterialProperties entity and assigned to an IfcMaterialDefinition.
